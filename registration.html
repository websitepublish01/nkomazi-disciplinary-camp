CAMP REGISTRATION 2026

Register for Mpumalanga Nkomazi Disciplinary Camp

Take the first step towards an organised camp experience focused on discipline, teamwork, responsibility and personal development.

Parents and guardians can use this form to submit participant information, select a camp package and provide the necessary registration details.

Before You Register

  • You have selected your preferred camp package.
  • You have the participant’s correct personal information.
  • Parent or guardian contact details are available.
  • You understand the registration fee of R500.
  • You have reviewed the camp’s registration requirements.
  • You are prepared to provide the required consent.

Registration fee R500, non-refundable. The organisers will confirm whether it is additional to the package price.

Online Registration Form

A. Participant Details

Please provide the personal information of the person attending the camp. We do not collect ID numbers.

B. Parent or Guardian Details

Provide the details of the parent or legal guardian responsible for the participant.

C. Emergency Contact Details

Provide another person who can be reached in an emergency, preferably someone other than the parent or guardian above.

D. Choose Your Preferred Package

Select the camp duration you would like to register for.

E. Important Participant Information

We ask only for information the organisers need to consider the participant’s safety and support. It is seen only by authorised staff.
