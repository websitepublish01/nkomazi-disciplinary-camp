Frequently Asked Questions

General Camp Information

What is Mpumalanga Nkomazi Disciplinary Camp?

A camp initiative focused on promoting discipline, teamwork, responsibility and personal development through organised camp experiences and structured activities.

What is the purpose of the camp?

To encourage positive behaviour, self-discipline, respect, teamwork and responsibility through structured activities and shared experiences.

Where is the camp located?

Our office is at Tonga Taxi Rank, Glorycity Mall, First Floor. Please contact the organisers to confirm the exact camp venue and directions.

When will the camp take place?

The advertised camp period is November and December 2026. Contact the organisers to confirm starting dates and programme schedules.

Who can attend the camp?

The camp is for participants interested in discipline, personal development and structured camp activities. Age requirements will be confirmed by the organisers.

Programmes and Activities

What activities will participants take part in?

The camp promotes teamwork and unity, discipline and security, structured training and camp experiences. Contact the organisers for the confirmed list of activities.

Does the camp include physical training?

Yes, structured training is advertised. Please contact the organisers to confirm the type, intensity and frequency of physical activities.

What does Teamwork and Unity involve?

Participants work together, communicate, respect one another and develop cooperation through group activities.

What does Discipline and Security mean?

This is one of the advertised focus areas. Contact the organisers to clarify whether it covers general discipline and supervision, security-related training or both.

Will participants receive a certificate?

Certificate availability and requirements will be confirmed by the camp organisers.

Registration and Requirements

How do I register for the camp?

Contact the organisers on WhatsApp or phone, 076 329 2437, or complete the online registration form on this website.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes. The advertised registration fee is R500 and it is non-refundable.

What information is required when registering?

Basic participant information, parent or guardian contact details and an emergency contact. The organisers will communicate any additional requirements.

Can a parent or guardian register a participant?

Yes. A parent or legal guardian should assist with registration where the participant is a minor and give the necessary consent.

Can I register more than one participant?

Please contact the organisers to confirm the process and any special arrangements.

Packages and Payments

What camp packages are available?

1 Week R1,500, 2 Weeks R2,500, 3 Weeks R3,500, 1 Month R5,000, 2 Months R10,000 and 3 Months R20,000. See the Camp Prices page.

Is the R500 registration fee included in the package price?

A separate R500 non-refundable registration fee is advertised. Please confirm with the organisers whether it is additional to the package price.

What is included in the package price?

Inclusions will be confirmed by the organisers. Before registering, ask whether the package covers accommodation, meals, training, activities, supervision or uniforms.

What payment methods are accepted?

Contact the organisers to confirm accepted payment methods, deadlines and whether instalments are available.

Safety and Parent/Guardian Information

How are participants supervised during the camp?

The organisers will provide information about supervision, responsible staff, emergency procedures and participant safety before registration is completed.

What should participants bring to the camp?

A confirmed packing list will be provided by the organisers.

What happens if a participant has a medical condition or special requirement?

Parents or guardians should tell the organisers before the camp begins so appropriate arrangements can be discussed.

Can parents or guardians contact participants during the camp?

Please contact the organisers to confirm communication arrangements, visiting rules and the contact person.

What happens if a participant can no longer attend?

The R500 registration fee is non-refundable. For package payments, withdrawals and refunds, ask for the camp’s full cancellation policy before paying.

Need More Information?

Our team is available to assist with enquiries about registration, camp packages, activities and other arrangements.
