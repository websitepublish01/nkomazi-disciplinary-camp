About Mpumalanga Nkomazi Disciplinary Camp

Discover our commitment to promoting discipline, teamwork, responsibility and positive character development among young people.

Who We Are

Mpumalanga Nkomazi Disciplinary Camp is a youth-focused initiative dedicated to encouraging discipline, teamwork, responsibility and personal development.

We provide structured camp experiences where young people take part in physical activities, teamwork exercises and organised training that promote positive values and responsible behaviour.

Through group participation, structured activities and outdoor camp experiences, we create opportunities to build confidence, cooperation and a stronger sense of responsibility.

Our approach is centred on discipline, respect, commitment and working together.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to provide young people with an organised camp environment that encourages positive character development through discipline, teamwork, physical activities and responsibility. We encourage participants to:

  • Develop self-discipline and respect.
  • Understand the importance of teamwork and unity.
  • Build confidence and personal responsibility.
  • Participate in structured physical activities.
  • Develop positive attitudes towards cooperation and commitment.

Our Mission

To encourage discipline, responsibility, teamwork and positive character development among young people through structured training, organised activities and meaningful camp experiences.

Our Vision

To contribute towards developing responsible, disciplined and confident young people who value teamwork, respect and positive participation in their communities.

How We Develop Discipline and Responsibility

We believe positive development comes through participation, consistency and structured activities. Our approach focuses on four key areas:

1. Structured Training

Organised physical activities that encourage discipline, commitment and responsibility.

2. Teamwork and Unity

Group activities that encourage cooperation, communication and mutual support.

3. Supervision and Support

A structured environment with supervision and guidance during camp activities.

4. Outdoor Camp Experiences

Outdoor activities and shared experiences that encourage personal development.
