Who We Are

Mpumalanga Nkomazi Disciplinary Camp is a youth-focused camp initiative designed to encourage discipline, teamwork, responsibility and positive character development.

Through structured physical training, outdoor camp experiences and group activities, participants develop important life values, build confidence and learn the importance of working together.

Our focus is an environment where young people can learn, grow and develop a stronger sense of responsibility.

Learn More About Us

Our Focus Areas

1. Teamwork & Unity

Youth building character together through cooperation, unity and group participation.

2. Discipline & Security

Professional supervision and support within a structured camp environment.

3. Structured Training

Physical discipline, commitment and personal responsibility.

4. Camp Experience

Outdoor camp experiences, group participation and community engagement.

Explore Our Programmes

More Than a Camp: An Experience in Personal Development

We believe that discipline, teamwork and responsibility are important values in personal development. Our camp is structured around activities that encourage young people to:

  • Develop self-discipline and responsibility.
  • Understand the importance of teamwork and unity.
  • Participate in structured physical activities.
  • Build confidence and positive character.
  • Learn cooperation and respect for others.
